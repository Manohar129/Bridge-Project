Common Queries.

How quickly can you deploy an engineering pod?

Our average deployment time is 48 to 72 hours. We maintain a "bench" of pre-vetted, senior engineers in Hyderabad who are ready to integrate into your Slack and Git workflows immediately.

Do you work in my time zone?

Yes. We operate on a Follow-the-Sun model. While our core hubs are in India (IST), our teams adjust their shifts to provide at least 4 hours of overlap with US/EU time zones for daily standups and handoffs.

What is your security protocol?

We are ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II compliant. All code is developed on secure VDI (Virtual Desktop Infrastructure) environments, ensuring no IP ever leaves the secure perimeter.

Can we scale the team down if needed?

Absolutely. Our contracts are designed for elasticity. You can scale your engineering headcount up or down with a 30-day notice period, allowing you to align costs with project velocity.
